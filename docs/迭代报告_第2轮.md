## 二、Pipeline Agent流程完整性

### 2.1 完整论文流程

```
选题 (TopicAgent)
    ↓
文献 (LiteratureAgent) → 输出: papers, paper_analyses, research_gaps
    ↓
Thesis凝练 (ThesisAgent) → 输出: thesis_statement, research_objectives
    ↓
大纲制定 (OutlineAgent) → 输出: outline (chapters, key_arguments)
    ↓
初稿撰写 (DraftWriterAgent) → 输出: full_draft
    ↓
修订编辑 (EditorAgent) → 输出: final_draft
    ↓
最终审核 (ReviewerAgent) → 输出: passed/failed, quality_score
```
